§ Selected work

Three systems, from production to research.

01 ● Live in production Conversational commerce · Moveo AI

A WhatsApp sales & service agent for a leading Israeli retailer

The retailer wanted customers to browse, get recommendations, and actually buy — inside WhatsApp, the channel they already live in. The hard constraint: an assistant that can quote real prices and stock, and never invents either.

The approach

  • Hybrid retrieval for product discovery — taxonomy expansion and SQL pre-filtering, then weighted fusion of BM25 + embeddings, then re-ranking.
  • An orchestration layer with tool-calling and reliable state management across multi-turn conversations.
  • Multi-stage output guardrails grounding every fact & price and stripping hallucinated product names — LLM decides what, code decides how.
  • Live data integration with product, inventory & pricing sources, with automated syncs keeping the catalog current.

The outcome

  • Shipped and live to real customers over WhatsApp.
  • Responses stay grounded and hallucination-free on prices and stock.
  • Catalog stays current automatically — no manual refresh.
  • Python
  • AWS Lambda
  • DynamoDB
  • S3
  • SQS FIFO
  • Hybrid RAG
  • LLM tool-calling
  • CI/CD
02 ● Live in production Customer service · Moveo AI

A customer-service bot for a national airline

High-volume, high-stakes support where a confidently wrong answer has real consequences for travelers. I contributed to the retrieval, orchestration, and guardrail layers that keep answers trustworthy at scale.

The approach

  • Retrieval and orchestration tuned for accuracy on sensitive, policy-bound queries.
  • Guardrails that keep the assistant grounded instead of improvising.
  • Deployed and monitored on serverless AWS with push-to-main CI/CD.

The outcome

  • In production for a national carrier.
  • Handles real support traffic with grounded, on-policy answers.
  • Python
  • RAG
  • LLM orchestration
  • Guardrails
  • Serverless AWS
03 ◆ Research · Side project B.Sc. final project · Azrieli College

An AI tutor that grades linear-algebra exercises, step by step

My degree's final project, and a question I wanted answered: could a smaller, open model actually check mathematical reasoning — giving step-by-step corrections, not just a final mark?

The approach

  • Fine-tuned WizardMath with LoRA to grade exercises and produce step-by-step corrections.
  • Built the data and training loop to teach the model how to evaluate a solution, not just solve one.

The outcome

  • A working agent that grades and corrects linear-algebra work.
  • Built within Level Up, a two-year honors program for outstanding students.
  • WizardMath
  • LoRA / PEFT
  • Fine-tuning
  • Python
